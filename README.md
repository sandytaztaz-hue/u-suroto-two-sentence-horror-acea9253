A two-sentence horror story for the [Two-Sentence Horror](https://make.fun/bounty/u-suroto-two-sentence-horror-acea9253) bounty on make.fun.

## The story

> My daughter drew our family for school today, and she drew three people. There are only two of us in this house, and the third one is holding my hand.

Exactly two sentences, 150 characters. The first sentence sets a warm, ordinary scene; the second turns it — the extra figure was already inside the house, and it is touching her hand.

## How it runs

`main.py` writes the story to `/output/entry.txt`:

```sh
python3 main.py
```

Deterministic: every run writes the same bytes.
